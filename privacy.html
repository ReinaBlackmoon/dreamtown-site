DreamTown — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026
Developer: ReinaLabs
App: DreamTown (com.dreamtown.game)
Contact: aifutureworld2025@gmail.com

Summary

DreamTown is a casual idle mayor game. We collect only the data needed to run the game, process optional in-app purchases, and (when enabled) online features such as chat and cloud save.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress (level, coins, gems, inventory, quests, settings)
  • Mayor name and cosmetic choices you create
  • Stored locally using Android app storage (SharedPreferences / app files)

This data stays on your device unless you use cloud backup (when available) or share information in online features.

Data we may process (online features, when enabled)

  • Google account identifier (for Sign in with Google / cloud save)
  • Display name you choose for your mayor
  • Chat messages (world, guild, or private chat)
  • Leaderboard statistics (level, popularity, gifts, faith, arcade scores)
  • Guild membership and contribution data

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase confirmation and product IDs to grant items in-game. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Data we do not collect

  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone (unless a future feature explicitly asks permission)
  • Health or financial account data beyond Play billing receipts

Children

DreamTown is intended for a general audience. If the app is listed for children under 13, we comply with applicable Google Play Families and COPPA-related requirements. Parents may contact us to request deletion of a child’s online data.

Your choices & rights

  • Delete local data: uninstall the app or clear app storage in Android Settings.
  • Cloud data: sign out of cloud save or email us to request deletion.
  • Opt out of chat: do not use social features; gameplay works offline.

Third-party services

  • Google Play (billing, app distribution)
  • Google Firebase (optional: auth, Firestore, analytics when enabled)

These providers have their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
